So the page a reader is on, read in another language, is the same path with the prefix swapped, and there is nothing to look up. That is what this does: a reader who reaches Lab 2.3 in English and clicks Français lands on Lab 2.3 in French, rather than back at the home page of the course, which is what the Zensical partial does. Two cases fall back to the home page of the language, which is what extra.alternate holds in course-site.yml: - a page with no language of its own (the backlog, the badges, a story page): it is written once, in English, and has no counterpart to jump to; - the home page of a language: the English one is the site home page, at the root rather than under /en/, so the prefix swap does not describe it. A language that ships an incomplete translation would send a reader into a 404 here. scripts/verify/check-nav.mjs is what says so, and check-site.mjs fails the build on the dead link, so nothing published can carry one.
